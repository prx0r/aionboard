# PROMISES.md

> What we promise each vertical. Work backwards from these to design the pipeline.

## The principle

**Don't sell the tool. Sell the outcome.**

They don't care about "AI" or "Meta Business Agent" or "schema markup." They care about:
- More bookings
- Fewer no-shows
- Less admin
- More customers

Every promise must be:
1. **Specific** — "no-shows reduced by 50%" not "fewer no-shows"
2. **Measurable** — we can prove it with data
3. **Time-bound** — "within 7 days" not "eventually"
4. **Risk-free** — if we don't deliver, they pay nothing

## The 1-week free trial

### What it is

- **Duration:** 7 days (not 4 weeks)
- **Cost:** £0
- **Commitment:** None — just stop if they don't like it
- **Setup:** 15 minutes (we do everything)
- **Risk:** Zero — no payment, no contract, no hardware

### What we do in the 15-minute setup

1. Turn on Meta Business Agent on their WhatsApp
2. Configure on their services, prices, hours
3. Add booking link to their Instagram/bio
4. Set up automated reminders
5. Configure review requests
6. Test with a fake enquiry
7. Show them how it works

### What they see in week 1

| Day | What happens |
|-----|-------------|
| Day 1 | AI goes live, starts answering enquiries |
| Day 2 | First bookings come through AI |
| Day 3 | Reminders fire, reviews requested |
| Day 4 | They see bookings in their diary |
| Day 5 | Weekly summary sent |
| Day 6 | They tell their friends |
| Day 7 | We ask: "Want to continue?" |

### What happens at day 7

**If they're happy:**
- "Great! It's £20 to keep it running. £50/month for ongoing support."
- They pay, we keep monitoring

**If they're not happy:**
- "No problem. Thanks for trying it. If you want it later, just reply."
- We turn off the AI, they keep their data

### Why 7 days, not 4 weeks

1. **Faster feedback** — we learn what works in 7 days, not 4 weeks
2. **Lower risk** — they commit to 7 days, not a month
3. **More trials** — we can do 4 batches in a month instead of 1
4. **Urgency** — "free for 7 days" is more compelling than "free for 4 weeks"
