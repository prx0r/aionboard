# Sales email 01 — first touch (Greater Manchester electricians)

> Send only to verified addresses with recorded permission or a valid soft opt-in. Never bulk-send to the research CSV. One email per business; stop on objection.

**Subject options (pick one, record which):**
- Quick question about [Business Name] enquiries
- 2 minutes on missed calls?

**Body:**

Hi [Name],

I came across [Business Name] while looking at electrical contractors in [Area]. Quick question — what happens to your enquiries when you're on the tools?

We do one thing: a £20 one-off setup that connects your existing booking, calendar, and WhatsApp so nothing slips through, with you approving everything before it goes out. No subscription, no new software to learn, no passwords shared — full details here: https://aionboard.co.uk

Worth a 10-minute call this week? I'm around [two specific times].

[Your Name]
AI Onboard — hello@aionboard.co.uk

---

**Follow-up (day 7, only if no reply and no objection):**

Hi [Name],

Bumping this once in case it got buried — still happy to do the 10-minute call if useful. If not, just say and I won't follow up again.

[Your Name]

---

## Rules (non-negotiable)

1. Verify the email address belongs to the business (website, directory, or prior contact).
2. Record source + date + legal basis in CRM before sending.
3. One follow-up maximum. Any objection (including silence after follow-up + 14 days) → suppression list.
4. Never attach anything. Never include pricing beyond the £20/£50 public offer.
5. Log outcome: replied / booked demo / not interested / no answer.
