| Gap | Evidence | Our answer |
|-----|----------|-----------|
| **AI is absent from most verticals** | 0/11 verticals have AI in their standard software | AI built-in at every tier |

| **Commission models are predatory** | Fresha 20%, Booksy 30%, Treatwell 35% | Zero commission, ever |

| **AI tools are separate/expensive** | £29-199/mo add-ons on top of existing software | AI included in £50/mo |

| **UK compliance is underserved** | CIS, EICR, RAMS, MTD still manual in most platforms | Compliance tracker add-on |
