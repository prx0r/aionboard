# FLYWHEEL.md

> Cheap network -> measured uplift -> verified proof -> cheaper acquisition -> paid tooling.

## The loop

1. Onboard cheap: one-fee AI-native setup, no subscription.
2. Measure before/after: snapshot baseline at join, re-measure at 14/30 days.
3. Publish verified proof: per-sector verified onboardings + reviews.
4. Grow network cheaper: proof lowers CAC, referrals compound.
5. Sell high-leverage extras: super-cheap tech support + custom tooling as loss-leader upsell.

## Before/after metrics

Capture at join and after onboarding:

- Calls answered %
- Missed-call leads captured
- No-show %
- Quote response time
- Quote close %
- Reviews count/rating
- Google Maps rank
- AI assistant citations
- Direct booking share
- Overdue invoice days

Store raw evidence, not vibes.

Publish per-sector proof pages. No proof, no claim.

## GTM: huge cheap network

- Cheap entry: £299 founding / £499 standard.
- Store details centrally: contact, services, tools, logins, workflows.
- Great service: 14-day fixes, plain-English handover.
- Then offer cheap ongoing value: tech support, lead alerts, custom builds.
- Loss-leader logic: setup buys trust and data; margin comes from repeatable extras and referrals.

## What not to do

- Don't promise subscriptions as the product.
- Don't invent uplift numbers.
- Don't mix customer PII into POW without explicit opt-in.
- Don't scale paid ads before referral proof exists.
