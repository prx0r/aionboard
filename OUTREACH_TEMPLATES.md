## Tips for cold calls

1. **Smile when you call** — it comes through in your voice
2. **Stand up** — more energy, more authority
3. **Have their website open** — reference something specific
4. **Ask questions, don't pitch** — "How do you currently handle X?"
5. **Be ready to book on the spot** — "I can set it up in 15 minutes. When works?"
6. **Take notes** — what they said, what they need, objections
7. **Follow up within 24 hours** — WhatsApp with a summary

## PECR compliance

Before any outreach:
1. Verify contact source (Google Maps = public, legitimate interest)
2. Record marketing permission or legal basis
3. Screen phone calls against TPS/CTPS
4. Respect opt-outs immediately
5. Identify yourself and your business
6. Include contact details in every message
