# Misha & Eti Beauty Studio — Onboarding

> First real customer. Manual-first. Learn everything.

## Onboarding session plan (30 minutes)

### Minute 0-5: Discovery

**What we ask:**
1. What booking system do you use? (Booksy, Fresha, Square, other?)
2. Can you show me your booking link?
3. What are your opening hours?
4. Do you take deposits? If so, how much?
5. Do you do patch tests? Which treatments require them?
6. What's your Instagram handle? (we know it's @nailsmanchester)
7. Do you have a Google Business Profile? Can you show me?

**What we record:**
- Booking system: ___
- Booking link: ___
- Hours: ___
- Deposit policy: ___
- Patch test treatments: ___
- GBP status: complete / incomplete

### Minute 5-10: Booking link setup

**What we do:**
1. Verify their booking link works
2. Help them add it to:
   - Instagram bio (we do this together)
   - Google Business Profile (we walk them through it)
   - Website header/footer
3. Test the full journey: find → click → book

**What we record:**
- Booking link: ___
- Platforms updated: Instagram □ Google □ Website □

### Minute 10-15: Google Business Profile completion

**What we check:**
- [ ] Business name correct
- [ ] Address complete
- [ ] Phone number correct
- [ ] Opening hours listed
- [ ] Website link added
- [ ] Services listed with prices
- [ ] 10+ photos uploaded
- [ ] Business description written
- [ ] Booking link added

**What we do:**
- Walk them through completing every field
- Help them add services + prices
- Tell them to upload 10+ photos this week

### Minute 15-20: AI visibility

**What we deliver:**
1. Schema markup (add to website header)
2. llms.txt (upload to website root)
3. robots.txt (upload to website root)
4. Instructions for adding all three

**What we explain:**
- "This makes your business visible when people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI"
- "It takes 5 minutes to add to your website"
- "No cost to you"

### Minute 20-25: Review request system

**What we set up:**
- WhatsApp template: "Thanks for visiting! If you loved your treatment, a quick Google review would mean a lot: [Google review link]"
- Tell them to send this after every appointment
- Expected: 2-3 new reviews per week

**What we give them:**
- The exact message to copy-paste
- Their Google review link (we find it)

### Minute 25-30: Training + handover

**What we show them:**
1. How the booking link works (test it together)
2. How to complete GBP (show them the fields)
3. How to send review requests (give them the template)
4. How to add schema/llms.txt (give them the files + instructions)

**What we give them:**
- schema.json file
- llms.txt file
- robots.txt file
- Review request template
- This document as handover

## Second session (after they give us access)

### What we need from them

| What | Why | How we get it |
|------|-----|---------------|
| Booking system login | To set up deposits, reminders, patch tests | Ask: "Can you show me your booking system? I need to configure a few things." |
| WhatsApp Business account | To set up automated messages | Ask: "Do you use WhatsApp Business? If not, I'll help you set it up." |
| Google Business Profile admin | To complete profile, add photos | Ask: "Can you log into your Google Business Profile? I'll guide you through it." |
| Instagram login | To add booking link to bio | Ask: "Can you open Instagram on your phone? I'll show you where to add it." |

### What we do in second session

1. **Set up deposits** in their booking system (if supported)
2. **Set up automated reminders** (48hr + 2hr before)
3. **Set up patch test flags** (if applicable)
4. **Complete GBP** (add all fields, photos, services)
5. **Add booking link to Instagram** (together on their phone)
6. **Verify schema/llms.txt** on their website
