# GO_TO_MARKET.md

> How to actually find and reach businesses for each vertical. Practical, not theoretical.

## The prospecting pipeline

```
Find businesses (Google Maps + directories)
  → Verify they're real (Companies House / TPS)
  → Score them (rating, reviews, website, activity)
  → Reach out (WhatsApp / email / phone / DM)
  → Convert to free onboarding (20 spots)
  → Measure and build case studies
  → Convert to paid (£20 → £50/mo)
```

- **Enrich:** Director names, incorporation date, SIC codes
- **Key insight:** 52% of businesses on Google Maps can't be matched to Companies House — they're sole traders. That's our market.

## Outreach methods (ranked by effectiveness)

| Method | Response rate | Best for | Notes |
|--------|-------------|----------|-------|
| **WhatsApp DM** | 40-60% | All verticals | Personal, immediate, where they already are |
| **Instagram DM** | 20-40% | Nails, beauty, hair, lashes | Visual businesses, DM is native |
| **Facebook message** | 15-30% | Cleaners, gardeners, dog groomers | Groups + pages |
| **Email** | 5-15% | Electricians, driving instructors | More formal, lower response |
| **Phone call** | 10-20% | Electricians, driving instructors | Intrusive but works for trades |

**Key insight:** WhatsApp is the highest-response channel for every vertical. It's where they already communicate with customers. Use it for outreach too.

## PECR compliance (UK law)

Before any outreach:
1. **Verify contact source** — Google Maps = public business data, legitimate interest
2. **Record permission basis** — Legitimate interest (PECR Reg 22(1)(b)) for B2B marketing
3. **Screen against TPS** — Before any phone calls
4. **Respect opt-outs** — "Reply STOP" honoured immediately
5. **Identify yourself** — "I'm [name] from AI Onboard"
6. **Include contact details** — Phone number + email in every message

**WhatsApp is NOT PECR-regulated the same way as email/SMS** — it's closer to a phone call. But GDPR still applies: lawful basis, transparency, right to object.
