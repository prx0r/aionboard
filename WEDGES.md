# WEDGES.md

> The 3 things that make businesses money. Per vertical.

## The wedges

| Wedge | What it does | Why they pay |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **Lead generation** | Find people who need their services RIGHT NOW | More customers = more money |
| **ChatGPT visibility** | Show up when people ask AI assistants | New channel they're not on |
| **Google search** | Show up higher in local search | More clicks, more calls |

## Per vertical

### NAILS

**Lead generation:**
- Monitor Instagram: #nailtech[city], #lookingfornails[city]
- Monitor TikTok: "nail tech recommendation" posts
- Monitor Reddit: r/[city] "nail tech" posts
- Monitor wedding venues: brides needing nails
- Deliverable: "Here are 5 people who need a nail tech this week"

**ChatGPT visibility:**
- Schema markup (LocalBusiness, BeautySalon)
- llms.txt with services, prices, hours
- Consistent NAP across Google, Instagram, website
- Deliverable: "Ask ChatGPT 'nail tech Manchester' — you now appear"

**Google search:**
- Complete GBP (service menu, photos, hours)
- Add 10+ photos
- Respond to every review
- Add weekly posts
- Deliverable: "Your Google profile went from 45% to 85%"
