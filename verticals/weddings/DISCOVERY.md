# Wedding supplier discovery

Make the business understandable to search engines and future consumer agents without promising placement.

Checklist:

1. Existing phone number retained unless the supplier asks to change it.
2. Consistent name, services, prices, hours, and service area.
3. Enquiry qualification and availability workflow documented.
4. Portfolio organized by service and location.
5. Structured website data where the supplier owns the website.
6. Optional `llms.txt` experiment.
7. Consistent listings across directories and wedding marketplaces the supplier owns.
8. Recent detailed reviews on platforms the supplier owns.
9. Approval-required behavior documented for every agent action.

Record account, date, method, screenshot/export, and pending verification status for every completed claim.
