# Wedding supplier pains

Research stub. These are discovery hypotheses, not measured customer outcomes.

- Enquiries arrive with incomplete dates, venues, party sizes, packages, and budgets.
- Availability changes faster than manual calendars and marketplace profiles.
- Proposals, deposits, contracts, and payment schedules require careful approval.
- Portfolio proof is scattered across social posts and galleries.
- Reviews are especially influential but inconsistently collected.

Do not present beauty research as wedding-photography evidence.
