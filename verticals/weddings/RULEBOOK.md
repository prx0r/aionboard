# Wedding supplier rulebook

> What "correct" means for this vertical and every way the AI gets it wrong. Built from research; must be validated against real jobs and updated after every mistake caught. This is the moat.

## How to use this file

Every time we deliver work for a wedding photographer or makeup artist customer and something goes wrong, add it here with the fix. After 50 jobs this list is worth more than the model. A competitor can copy our tools; they can't copy this.

---

## Lead qualification (DISCOVERY-QUALIFICATION)

| # | What goes wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|----------------|----------------|-----|
| 1 | Enquiry lacks date, venue, or budget | Supplier can't quote or check availability, wastes time on unqualified leads | Capture: date, venue, party size, package interest, budget range — before quoting |
| 2 | AI quotes without checking availability | Couple gets excited, supplier is already booked | Check calendar availability before any quote |
| 3 | Enquiry from wrong year captured as current | Couple messages in 2026 for a 2027 wedding, AI treats as immediate | Always confirm the wedding year; flag if more than 18 months out |
| 4 | Budget not captured, quote overshoots | Supplier sends £3,000 package to couple with £800 budget, wastes both parties' time | Ask budget range during qualification; only quote within range or explain gap |
| 5 | Multi-supplier enquiry treated as single-supplier | Couple asks about photography + makeup, AI only responds about one | Detect multi-service enquiries; ask which services they need |
| 6 | Weekend availability not cross-referenced | Saturday is peak day; couple is told "yes" without checking the actual date | Cross-reference with calendar; peak-season Saturdays are the most constrained |
| 7 | Enquiry labelled "booked" when they only asked about pricing | Pipeline data is wrong, follow-up never happens | Enquiry stages: new → qualified → quoted → proposal_sent → deposit_paid → booked → completed |

## Proposal process (DISCOVERY-PROPOSAL)

| # | What goes wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|----------------|----------------|-----|
| 8 | Proposal sent without contract terms | Couple accepts, no deposit collected, supplier has no protection | Every proposal includes: package, price, deposit amount, cancellation terms, balance due date |
| 9 | Deposit amount too low for high-ticket booking | Couple backs out, supplier loses a £2,000 date | Deposit at 25-40% for bookings over £500; minimum £100 |
| 10 | Proposal doesn't show what's included | Couple compares apples to oranges, picks cheaper option that included less | List exactly what's included: hours, edits, prints, trial session, second shooter, etc. |
| 11 | Proposal expires without deadline | Couple sits on it for 3 months, supplier holds the date, loses other enquiries | State proposal validity (14-21 days typical); remind before expiry |
| 12 | Contract terms not sent with proposal | Couple says yes verbally, no written agreement, disputes later | Attach contract or link to terms with every proposal |
| 13 | Cancellation terms unclear | Couple cancels 2 months before wedding, supplier can't rebook the date | State cancellation terms clearly: deposit non-refundable, balance due 30 days before |
| 14 | Proposal sent without portfolio samples | Couple can't see work quality, goes to supplier with better portfolio | Include 3-5 relevant portfolio images or a link to portfolio in every proposal |

## Seasonal and availability

| # | What goes wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|----------------|----------------|-----|
| 15 | Peak-season enquiries not prioritised | Saturday in June is worth 3x a Tuesday in November | Flag peak-season dates (May-September, Saturdays); prioritise response |
| 16 | Date held without deposit after verbal yes | Couple says yes, supplier holds date, couple ghosts, date is lost | Maximum 48hr hold without deposit; after that, release the date |
| 17 | Travel to venue not priced | Venue is 2 hours away, supplier absorbs the cost | Capture venue location during intake; include travel in proposal for distant venues |
| 18 | Second shooter or assistant not accounted for | Supplier books solo, couple expected a second shooter | Confirm team requirements per package; include in proposal if applicable |

## Communication and trust

| # | What goes wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|----------------|----------------|-----|
| 19 | AI responds faster than the supplier normally would | Couple notices inconsistency, feels like talking to a bot | Maintain consistent response patterns; don't over-promise speed |
| 20 | Consultation not scheduled after initial enquiry | Couple forgets, supplier loses momentum | Auto-suggest consultation within 48h of qualified enquiry |
| 21 | Mood board or style preferences not captured | Supplier delivers dark moody photos, couple wanted bright and airy | Ask about style preferences during qualification; note in proposal |
| 22 | Venue details not captured for logistics | Supplier arrives without knowing parking, setup space, timeline | Capture venue name, address, access notes, timeline during booking |
| 23 | No follow-up after consultation | Couple meets supplier, likes them, forgets to book | Follow up within 24h of consultation; remind at 48h and 7 days |

## Pricing and payments

| # | What goes wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|----------------|----------------|-----|
| 24 | Package pricing not clearly differentiated | Couple confused about what Basic vs Premium includes | Present packages with clear differences; use comparison format |
| 25 | Add-ons not priced upfront | Couple expects album, engagement shoot, extra hours included | List all add-ons with prices in proposal; customer selects before confirming |
| 26 | Balance due date not enforced | Supplier chases balance on wedding morning, awkward | Balance due 30 days before wedding; auto-remind at 45d, 30d, 14d |
| 27 | VAT not stated on proposals for VAT-registered businesses | Couple expects £2,000 inc VAT, invoice is £2,400 | Always state whether prices are inc or ex VAT; match to registration status |

## Safety and compliance

| # | What goes wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|----------------|----------------|-----|
| 28 | Image consent not obtained in writing | Supplier uses wedding photos for marketing, couple objects | Get written image consent in contract; opt-out available |
| 29 | Under-18 bridesmaid/flower girl photos without guardian consent | Safeguarding issue with images of minors | Require guardian consent for photos of under-18s at wedding |
| 30 | Makeup patch test not recorded for bridal party | Allergic reaction on wedding day, liability | Require patch-test confirmation before first use of new product on any bridal party member |
| 31 | Data protection: couple's personal data shared without consent | Trust issue, legal exposure | Couple data shared only with explicit permission; never with other suppliers without consent |

## General

| # | What goes wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|----------------|----------------|-----|
| 32 | AI promises availability on a peak Saturday without checking | Couple plans around a date that's already booked | Always check calendar before confirming availability |
| 33 | AI promises turnaround times on photos | "You'll have your photos in 2 weeks" — supplier takes 8 weeks | Never promise delivery timelines; state supplier's typical turnaround if known |
| 34 | Handover doesn't explain how to update packages | Supplier changes packages, AI still quotes old ones | Handover includes: "To update packages, edit [file] and tell us" |
| 35 | Support window expires but supplier still needs help | Wedding season is intense; 7-day window too short | Track support usage; flag if no contact by day 5 |

## Review cadence

- After every booking: check if anything went wrong. If yes, add to this list.
- Weekly: review all entries, remove any that are no longer relevant.
- After 50 bookings: this list is the product. Protect it.
