# Wedding supplier stack

Discover and use the supplier’s existing enquiry, calendar, contract, payment, portfolio, marketplace, and messaging tools. Do not force migration or invent a stack.

Makeup work may use the beauty stack. Photography tooling must be discovered per supplier.

Live voice, WhatsApp production integration, custom builds, and new websites are separate deliverables. Outbound proposals, deposits, contracts, payments, and customer messages require explicit approval.
