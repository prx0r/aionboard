## Lead qualification flow (WhatsApp)

```
"Hi, I'm getting married in June and looking for a photographer"
  ↓
AI: "Congratulations! What's your date? Where's the venue?"
  ↓
AI checks calendar → "I'm available! Here's my portfolio: [link]"
  ↓
AI: "My packages start at £1,200 for [X hours].
     Would you like to see the full brochure?"
  ↓
If interested: "Let me send you a questionnaire to understand your day better"
  ↓
AI sends questionnaire (Google Form or Typeform):
  - Date, venue, ceremony time
  - Number of guests
  - Bride/groom names
  - Key moments to capture
  - Style preferences (posed, candid, documentary)
  - Budget range
  ↓
AI drafts proposal → photographer reviews → sends
  ↓
AI follows up at day 2/5/12 if no response
```

## Wedding day WhatsApp flow

```
Morning: "Good morning [couple]! Excited for your day.
          I'll arrive at [time] at [venue]. See you there!"
  ↓
At venue: GPS check-in, first shots
  ↓
During: "Sneak peek for you!" (1–2 edited photos sent to couple)
  ↓
After: "It was an honour to capture your day. Sneak peek attached!"
  ↓
1 week: Full gallery delivered
  ↓
1 month: "Would you mind leaving a review? [Google link]"
  ↓
6 months: "Happy half-year! Here's a print credit for your anniversary"
```

## Contract + deposit flow

```
Proposal accepted
  ↓
AI sends contract (e-sign via DocuSign or HelloSign)
  ↓
Contract includes:
  - Date, venue, hours of coverage
  - Package details + add-ons
  - Payment schedule: 25% deposit, 50% at 3 months, 25% 14 days before
  - Cancellation policy
  - Deliverables + timeline
  ↓
Deposit link sent with contract
  ↓
AI tracks: signed? deposit paid? next payment due?
  ↓
Auto-remind at each milestone
```

## Supplier referral flow

```
Completed wedding
  ↓
AI logs: venue, florist, caterer, DJ/band, planner
  ↓
AI sends thank-you to each supplier with gallery link
  ↓
AI: "Would you like to be listed on my preferred supplier page?"
  ↓
If yes: mutual backlinks = SEO boost
  ↓

```

## What the top 10% do differently

- Send a sneak peek within 24 hours of the wedding — not a week later
- Include a "first look" session in every package — emotional content = shares
- Offer a "day-after" mini session (£200) — easy upsell, different light
- Create a private Instagram highlight for each wedding — couples share it
- Send a printed thank-you card after delivery — costs £2, worth £200 in referrals
- Build a "preferred supplier" page on their website — mutual backlinks + trust
- Offer a 10% referral discount to every past couple — turns clients into salespeople
