# Lash and brow stack

Use the customer’s existing booking and payment tools. Integrate or import; do not force migration.

Relevant existing tools include Booksy, Fresha, Square Appointments, GlossGenius, Vagaro, Mangomint, and customer exports. Live voice, WhatsApp production integration, custom builds, and new websites are separate deliverables.

Client photos, allergy records, and preferences are personal data. Never publish identifiable images without explicit written consent. Outbound bookings, deposits, payments, and customer messages require explicit approval.
