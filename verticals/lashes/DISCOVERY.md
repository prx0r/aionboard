# Lash and brow discovery

Make the business understandable to search engines and future consumer agents without promising placement.

Checklist:

1. Existing phone number retained unless the customer asks to change it.
2. Consistent name, treatments, prices, hours, and service area.
3. Booking link present in Instagram/TikTok bios and Google profile where applicable.
4. Treatment preparation and patch-test requirements stated plainly.
5. Structured website data where the customer owns the website.
6. Optional `llms.txt` experiment.
7. Consistent listings across directories the customer owns.
8. Recent detailed reviews on platforms the customer owns.
9. Approval-required behavior documented for every agent action.

Record account, date, method, screenshot/export, and pending verification status for every completed claim.
