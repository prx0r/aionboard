# Lash and brow pains

Research state, not measured customer outcomes.

1. Appointment preparation is safety-critical.
   - Patch tests and allergy records must precede relevant treatments.
   - Missing records create clinical and reputational risk.

2. Social DMs replace a booking system.
   - Availability, preparation instructions, deposits, and confirmations are handled manually.

3. Repeat demand is not systematically rebooked.
   - Lash fills and brow maintenance have natural intervals.
   - Consent-based reminders are required.

Also track no-shows, deposits, portfolio updates, and review collection.
