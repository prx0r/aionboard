# Gardener and window-cleaner stack

Use the customer’s existing round, scheduling, payment, and messaging tools. Integrate or import; do not force migration.

Relevant window-cleaner tools include Cleaner Planner, Work Planner, Manage My Round, Squeegee/George/Aworka, GoCardless, Google Calendar, and customer exports. Gardener tooling must be discovered per customer.

Live voice, WhatsApp production integration, custom builds, and new websites are separate deliverables. Outbound scheduling, payments, and customer messages require explicit approval.
