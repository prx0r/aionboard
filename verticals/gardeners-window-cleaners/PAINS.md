# Gardener and window-cleaner pains

Research state, not measured customer outcomes.

1. Unlogged skips and unchased debt.
   - Round economics depend on skip, credit, and debt discipline.

2. Lapsed round customers are not re-canvassed.
   - Streets already know the van or crew, making local reactivation efficient in principle.

3. Paper rounds are a single point of failure.
   - Customer, visit, skip, payment, and debt records need a durable system.

Gardeners need additional discovery for seasonal work, access, waste removal, equipment, and weather disruption. Do not present window-cleaner evidence as gardener evidence.
