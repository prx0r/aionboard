# Electrician campaign

Narrow paid pilot adapted from `CAMP-ELECTRICIAN-GB`.

## Qualified prospect

Owner-managed electrical business with recurring admin work and an owner still involved in enquiries, quotes, or scheduling.

Verify Companies House status, public contact source, and marketing permission before outreach. Screen live calls against TPS, CTPS, and suppression.

## Paid wedge

Basic onboarding (£20):

1. Existing email and calendar setup.
2. Enquiry triage into a job record with photos and address.
3. Quote draft from the approved price book.
4. Owner approval before sending.
5. Scheduling proposal from availability and travel constraints.
6. Completed-job record with final price and duration.
7. Invoice preparation in customer-owned accounting software.
8. Review request after payment.
9. Training and written handover.

## Separately quoted

- Website or AI chat build.
- Live voice service.
- Meta production integration.
- Custom accounting or job-system integration.
- Lead generation.

## Demonstration

Use only fictional customer data. Clearly mark simulated integrations and manual steps. Demonstrate:

- Fictional enquiry intake.
- Job classification.
- Quote draft from an approved price book.
- Human approval.
- Scheduling proposal.
- Handover evidence format.
