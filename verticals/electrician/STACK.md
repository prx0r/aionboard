# Electrician stack

Use the customer’s existing software where possible. Import data rather than replacing systems unnecessarily.

## Job management: import from

- Tradify
- Fergus
- Housecall Pro
- Jobber
- ServiceTitan
- Commusoft
- ServiceM8
- Powered Now, especially for UK compliance and offline work

Pilot workflow imports customers, price book, and job history where the customer’s export and permissions allow.

## Accounting: integrate with customer-owned accounts

- Xero, QuickBooks, or Sage
- CIS deductions and reverse-charge VAT remain in accounting software.
- AI Onboard does not move money without explicit approval.

## Communications: existing number first

- Keep the customer’s existing phone number unless they explicitly request a change.
- Telnyx plus a voice agent is a separate voice-service deliverable.
- WhatsApp production integration requires Meta access, business verification, payment terms, and explicit authorization.

## Evidence rules

- Unknown job types quote a site visit plus human review.
- Never invent prices.
- Outbound quotes, bookings, payments, and customer messages require owner approval.
