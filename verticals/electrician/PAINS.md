# Electrician pains

Research state, not measured customer outcomes.

## Top teardown pains

1. Missed calls while on the tools.
   - `FIX-MISSED-INBOUND`
   - Teardown language: trades miss the majority of calls at height.
   - Basis: template analysis.
   - Do not quote a universal missed-call percentage as fact.

2. Extras done on a nod and never billed.
   - `FIX-UNPRICED-EXTRAS`
   - High-frequency margin leak on smaller tickets.

3. Certification coverage gaps discovered at the worst moment.
   - `FIX-COMPLIANCE-DRIFT`
   - EICR, Gas Safe, and Part P competence must be checked per job type.

## Margin leaks

- Missed inbound enquiry goes to the next available trade.
- Retail materials, flat quotes, and unbilled waste reduce margin.
- Sent quotes are not followed up.

## Acquisition reality

- Word of mouth, Checkatrade, and Google.
- Direct inbound calls, SMS, and WhatsApp.
- Letting agents and landlords supply repeat commercial work.
- Reviews strongly affect referral and repeat business.

## Compliance spine

- Uncertified work on notifiable jobs can invalidate insurance.
- CIS verification and deductions belong in accounting, not informal notes.

## Metrics to baseline before installation

- Answered-call percentage
- Missed-call leads captured
- Quote response time
- Quote close percentage
- Review count and rating
- Certificate-expiry coverage
