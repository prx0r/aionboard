# Mobile hair pains

Research state, not measured customer outcomes.

1. Social DMs replace a booking system.
   - Service, location, travel, timing, and price are negotiated manually.

2. Service-area ambiguity wastes enquiries.
   - Customers do not know where the professional travels.
   - Travel time and costs are inconsistently handled.

3. Repeat demand is not systematically rebooked.
   - Maintenance, colour refreshes, and reinstalls have natural intervals.
   - Portfolio proof is scattered across social posts.

Also track deposits, no-shows, patch-test requirements for colour, and review collection.
