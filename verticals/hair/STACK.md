# Mobile hair stack

Use the customer’s existing booking, calendar, payment, and social tools. Integrate or import; do not force migration.

Relevant existing tools include Booksy, Fresha, Square Appointments, Google Calendar, Stripe or Square payments, and customer exports. Live voice, WhatsApp production integration, custom builds, and new websites are separate deliverables.

Do not invent prices. Outbound bookings, deposits, payments, and customer messages require explicit approval.
