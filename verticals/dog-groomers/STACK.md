# Dog groomer stack

Use the customer’s existing booking, payment, route, and marketplace tools. Integrate or import; do not force migration.

Relevant existing tools include Time To Pet, Dog Walker Central, TendPets, Rover, Wag, PetBacker, Google Calendar, and customer-owned payment tools. Live voice, WhatsApp production integration, custom builds, and new websites are separate deliverables.

Pet medical and behavior records are sensitive. Outbound bookings, deposits, payments, and customer messages require explicit approval.
