# Dog groomer campaign

Qualified prospect: dog groomer with recurring appointments and repeatable services.

Screen contact source, permission, TPS/CTPS, and suppression before outreach.

Paid pilot covers existing email/calendar workflows, recurring booking workflow, medical and behavior-record organization, deposit and reminder preparation, waiting-list workflow, review workflow, Google Business Profile assistance, training, and handover.

Demonstrate with fictional customer and pet data only. Clearly mark simulated integrations and manual steps.
