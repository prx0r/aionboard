# Dog groomer pains

Research state, not measured customer outcomes.

1. Marketplace fees on repeat customers.
   - Research describes Rover and Wag commissions and the value of direct repeat booking.

2. Lapsed owners are not re-engaged.
   - Dogs need recurring grooming; lapses are usually scheduling failures.

3. Medical and behavior flags live in people’s heads.
   - Missing protocols create safety and trust risk.

Also track deposits, no-shows, waiting lists, drive time, and review collection.
