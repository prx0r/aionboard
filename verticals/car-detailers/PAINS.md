# Mobile car detailer pains

Research stub. These are discovery hypotheses, not measured customer outcomes.

- Quote requests arrive with incomplete vehicle, location, condition, and timing details.
- Photo-based pricing needs clear approval boundaries.
- Appointment scheduling competes with travel and weather constraints.
- Repeat services are not systematically offered.
- Reviews and portfolio proof are inconsistently collected.

Do not present adjacent garage or trade research as car-detailer evidence.
