# Nail technician campaign

Qualified prospect: independent or home-based nail technician with social-led enquiries and repeatable services.

Screen contact source, permission, TPS/CTPS, and suppression before outreach.

Paid pilot covers existing email/calendar workflows, booking-link and deposit preparation, social enquiry triage, reminders, rebooking preparation, review workflow, Google Business Profile assistance, training, and handover.

Demonstrate with fictional customer data only. Clearly mark simulated integrations and manual steps.
