# Nail technician pains

Research state, not measured customer outcomes.

1. Social DMs replace a booking system.
   - Enquiries arrive on Instagram/TikTok/WhatsApp.
   - Availability, prices, deposits, and confirmations are handled manually.

2. No-shows and late cancellations.
   - Long appointments carry material and time costs.
   - Deposits and reminders are the research-backed countermeasure.

3. Repeat demand is not systematically rebooked.
   - Infills and maintenance have natural intervals.
   - Lapsed clients are rarely re-engaged.

Also track review collection, portfolio updates, deposit disputes, and patch/allergy notes where relevant.
