## The offer (outcome-based)

**Standard AI Setup — £499 one-off:**

Every appointment over £30 has a deposit. No-shows drop from 8% to under 3%. Enquiries go to booking instead of DM ping-pong. Every completed appointment gets a review request. Lapsed customers get rebooking reminders at their infill interval.

**Muse Quickstart — £20 one-off:**

One priority workflow: bookings, enquiries, quotations, or appointment admin. Training + manual + 7 days of support.

Canonical pricing is in `../../OFFER.md`.

## Rulebook

37 failure modes across 7 categories. Updated after every job. This is the moat — a competitor can copy our tools, they can't copy this. See `RULEBOOK.md`.
