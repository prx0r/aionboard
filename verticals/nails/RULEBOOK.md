# Nail technician rulebook

> What "correct" means for this vertical and every way the AI gets it wrong. Built from research; must be validated against real jobs and updated after every mistake caught. This is the moat.

## How to use this file

Every time we deliver work for a nail tech customer and something goes wrong, add it here with the fix. After 50 jobs this list is worth more than the model. A competitor can copy our tools; they can't copy this.

---

## No-show prevention (PP-008)

| # | What goes wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|----------------|----------------|-----|
| 1 | Deposit amount doesn't match service length | Tech loses a 90-min appointment for £5 when the seat costs £40+ | Set deposits at 20-30% of service value, minimum £10 |
| 2 | Deposit deadline too late | Customer books, never pays, no-shows | Deposit due within 2 hours of booking, auto-cancel after |
| 3 | Reminder sent too early | Customer forgets by appointment day | Send 48hr + 2hr reminders, not 7 days |
| 4 | Reminder tone too formal | Feels like a bank, not a salon | Friendly tone: "See you Thursday! Quick reminder — your gel infill is at 2pm" |
| 5 | No cancellation policy stated upfront | Customer disputes deposit when they cancel late | State cancellation window in booking confirmation (24hr or 48hr) |
| 6 | No-show fee not enforced | Tech eats the cost, learns nothing | Auto-charge no-show fee after grace period; log it |
| 7 | Repeat no-shower not flagged | Same person keeps wasting slots | Track no-show rate per customer; flag at 2+ no-shows |

## DM triage and booking (PP-002)

| # | What goes wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|----------------|----------------|-----|
| 8 | Quick reply sends before customer finishes typing | Feels robotic, answers wrong question | Only trigger quick reply after message is complete |
| 9 | Price quote doesn't match actual service | Customer shows up expecting £25, price is £45 | Pull prices from approved price book only |
| 10 | Availability shown for wrong location | Tech works from home on certain days, salon on others | Link availability to location/service, not just calendar |
| 11 | Deposit link sent without service confirmation | Customer confused about what they're depositing for | Confirm service + time + price before sending deposit link |
| 12 | Instagram DM enquiry answered, TikTok DM missed | enquiries split across platforms, some fall through | All DMs route to same triage; if not possible, document which platforms are monitored |
| 13 | After-hours enquiry auto-replies with "we'll get back to you" but nobody follows up | Customer goes to next tech | Auto-reply acknowledges; next-morning follow-up is mandatory |
| 14 | Enquiry labelled "booked" when they only asked about price | Pipeline data is wrong, follow-up never happens | Enquiry stages: new → quoted → deposit_paid → booked → completed |

## Rebooking and win-back (PP-037)

| # | What goes wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|----------------|----------------|-----|
| 15 | Win-back sent too early | Customer just had nails done, feels spammed | Wait minimum 2 weeks before rebooking reminder |
| 16 | Win-back sent too late | Customer already booked elsewhere | Send at typical infill interval (2-3 weeks for gel, 4-6 for acrylic) |
| 17 | Win-back message is generic | "Hey, haven't seen you!" — no personal touch | Reference last service: "Your gel set from 3 weeks ago — time for a refresh?" |
| 18 | No infill interval tracked per service type | Can't predict when customer needs rebooking | Map each service to its interval: gel=2-3wk, acrylic=3-4wk, dipping=3wk |
| 19 | Win-back sent without approval | Tech didn't want to message that customer | Every outbound win-back requires owner approval |
| 20 | Rebooking link goes to wrong booking page | Customer clicks, can't book, gives up | Verify booking link works for the specific service before including |

## Reviews and reputation (PP-060)

| # | What goes wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|----------------|----------------|-----|
| 21 | Review request sent before service is complete | Customer hasn't seen final result, rates low | Send review request 24hr after appointment, not during |
| 22 | Review request goes to wrong platform | Tech cares about Google, request goes to Facebook | Ask which platform matters most; send there only |
| 23 | No response to negative review | Bad review sits unanswered, looks like tech doesn't care | Respond within 24hr: acknowledge, offer to make it right, take offline |
| 24 | Review request doesn't mention what to include | "Great service!" — no detail, no SEO value | Suggest: "Mention the service, the location, and what you liked" |
| 25 | Same review request template every time | Feels spammy, lower response rate | Vary the message; reference the actual service |

## Pricing and payments

| # | What goes wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|----------------|----------------|-----|
| 26 | Price book outdated | AI quotes old prices, tech has to correct, customer annoyed | Verify price book monthly; flag when last updated |
| 27 | Deposit doesn't cover material cost for complex sets | Tech loses money on heavily-customised work | Higher deposits for nail art, chrome, 3D designs |
| 28 | Travel charge not included for mobile techs | Quoted £35, actual is £45 with travel | Include travel in quote if mobile; state it explicitly |
| 29 | Group booking deposit is per-person, not per-group | 4 people book, only 1 deposit paid | Calculate deposit per person for group bookings |

## Safety and compliance

| # | What goes wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|----------------|----------------|-----|
| 30 | Patch test not recorded before new product | Allergic reaction, liability | Require patch-test confirmation before first use of new product |
| 31 | Allergy info not passed to booking notes | Tech uses acrylic on acrylic-allergic customer | Link allergy notes to customer profile; flag on booking |
| 32 | COSHH records not maintained | Legal requirement, not optional | Track product safety data sheets; flag when missing |
| 33 | Under-18 appointment without guardian consent | Safeguarding issue | Flag under-18 bookings; require guardian contact |

## General

| # | What goes wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|----------------|----------------|-----|
| 34 | AI promises something tech doesn't offer | Customer arrives expecting service that isn't available | Only reference services in approved service menu |
| 35 | AI promises same-day booking when diary is full | Customer frustrated, trust broken | Check availability before confirming |
| 36 | Handover doesn't explain how to update prices | Tech changes prices, AI still quotes old ones | Handover includes: "To update prices, edit [file] and tell us" |
| 37 | Support window expires but tech still needs help | 7-day window too short for some | Track support usage; flag if no contact by day 5 |
