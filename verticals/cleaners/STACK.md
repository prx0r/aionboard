# Domestic cleaner stack

Use the customer’s existing booking, scheduling, payment, and messaging tools. Integrate or import; do not force migration.

Relevant existing tools include ZenMaid, BookingKoala, Launch27, ConvertLabs, Cleenie, ProCleanerUK, Swept, Jobber, Google Calendar, and customer-owned payment tools. Live voice, WhatsApp production integration, custom builds, and new websites are separate deliverables.

Do not invent prices. Outbound scheduling, quotes, payments, and customer messages require explicit approval.
