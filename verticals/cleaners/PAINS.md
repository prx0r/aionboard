# Domestic cleaner pains

Research state, not measured customer outcomes.

1. Paused or cancelled plans are never won back.
   - Research describes recoverable monthly revenue leaking away.

2. Travel, materials, and deep-clean extras go unbilled.
   - Fixed prices absorb variable costs.

3. Payments and repeat scheduling require manual chasing.
   - Payment reminders, plan changes, and rebooking consume owner time.

Also track quote follow-up, review collection, rota gaps, and tooling costs.
