# Driving instructor discovery

Make the business understandable to search engines and future consumer agents without promising placement.

Checklist:

1. Existing phone number retained unless the instructor asks to change it.
2. Consistent name, services, prices, hours, and service area.
3. Lesson and waiting-list enquiry workflow documented.
4. Booking or enquiry link present where applicable.
5. Structured website data where the instructor owns the website.
6. Optional `llms.txt` experiment.
7. Consistent listings across directories the instructor owns.
8. Recent detailed reviews on platforms the instructor owns.
9. Approval-required behavior documented for every agent action.

Record account, date, method, screenshot/export, and pending verification status for every completed claim.
