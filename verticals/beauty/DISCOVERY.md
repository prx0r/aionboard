# Beauty discovery

Goal: make the business understandable to search engines and future consumer agents without promising placement.

## Portable readiness checklist

1. Existing phone number retained unless the customer asks to change it.
2. Consistent business name, address, phone, treatments, and service area.
3. Completed Google Business Profile fields through Google’s ordinary interface.
4. Treatment menu with durations, skill requirements, deposits, and cancellation terms.
5. Structured website data where the customer owns the website.
6. Optional `llms.txt` experiment.
7. Bing verification where applicable.
8. Consistent listings across directories the customer owns.
9. Recent detailed reviews on platforms the customer owns.
10. Approval-required behavior documented for every agent action.

## Consumer-agent posture

Do not design the pilot around unconfirmed Meta Muse behavior.

Accept future consumer-agent contact through channels the customer owns and approves. Maintain human approval for bookings, deposits, payments, and outbound messages. Treat website creation, live voice, and Meta production integration as separate deliverables.

## Evidence

Record every completed discovery claim with the account, date, method, screenshot or export, and pending verification status. A submitted request is not proof of indexing, verification, or recommendation.
