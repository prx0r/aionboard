# Beauty pains

Research state, not measured customer outcomes.

## Top teardown pains

1. Calls during treatments go to voicemail.
   - `FIX-MISSED-INBOUND`
   - Research describes a category-wide reception gap.
   - Basis: research analysis, not a measured pilot outcome.

2. No-shows on staffed rooms.
   - `FIX-NOSHOW`
   - Deposits and reminders are the research-backed countermeasure.

3. Package breakage and lapsed clients are not worked.
   - `FIX-SILENT-CHURN`
   - Prepaid value and repeat demand leak in both directions.

## Margin and risk issues

- Marketplace discovery can carry new-client fees.
- Add-on software costs can accumulate.
- Missed allergy or patch-test records create safety risk.
- Client photos and preferences are personal data under UK GDPR.

## Acquisition reality

- Marketplace discovery through Fresha, Vagaro, Booksy, or similar platforms.
- Instagram and referrals.
- Repeat bookings secured through packages and rebooking discipline.

## Metrics to baseline before installation

- Missed-call or missed-message count
- No-show percentage
- Repeat-booking percentage
- Package sell-through and redemption
- Review count and rating
- Patch-test and allergy-record coverage
