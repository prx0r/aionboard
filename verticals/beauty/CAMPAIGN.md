# Beauty campaign

Narrow paid pilot adapted from `CAMP-BEAUTY-GB`.

## Qualified prospect

Owner-managed salon, nail service, makeup service, or small beauty business with recurring bookings and an owner involved in enquiries, scheduling, or follow-up.

Verify business status, public contact source, and marketing permission before outreach. Screen live calls against TPS, CTPS, and suppression.

## Paid wedge

Standard £499 pilot installation:

1. Existing email and calendar setup.
2. Booking-request triage with treatment, staff, room, patch-test, and allergy checks.
3. Deposit and reminder workflow preparation within the customer’s existing booking platform.
4. Win-back list preparation from customer-authorized exports.
5. Owner approval before sending.
6. Google Business Profile assistance through Google’s ordinary interface.
7. Training and written handover.

## Separately quoted

- Website or AI chat build.
- Live voice service.
- Meta production integration.
- Custom booking, payment, or package integration.
- Lead generation.
